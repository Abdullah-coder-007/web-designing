Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

What we collect

When you request a free concept or email us, we collect the details you give us: your name, business name, email address, website address, country and any notes. We also receive basic technical data from our hosting provider, such as the country your request came from.

How we use it

  • To prepare and send your free homepage concept and quote.
  • To reply to your questions and, if you become a client, to deliver your project.
  • To keep records of our business communications.

We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to a newsletter unless you ask us to.

Business emails we send

We sometimes contact businesses whose email address is publicly listed, with a message relevant to their website. Every such email says who we are, gives a way to contact us, and lets you opt out. If you reply asking us to stop, we won't contact you again.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, and may be forwarded to our email inbox. These providers may store data outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to two years, and client project records for as long as needed for tax and legal reasons.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Email hello@kromaloom.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers your chosen currency in your own browser, which you can clear at any time.
