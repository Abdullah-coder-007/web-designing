What an outdated website is quietly costing you

Every one of these turns visitors away, and several of them also count against you on Google.

It loads slowly on phones

53% of mobile visitors leave a page that takes more than 3 seconds to load.

On Google
Loading speed is part of Google's page experience signals, through Core Web Vitals.

Sources: Google, The Need for Mobile Speed, 2016, Google Search Central

It breaks on mobile

Google now looks at the mobile version of your site first when it decides where you rank.

On Google
If your phone layout is cramped or missing content, that is the version Google judges.

Source: Google Search Central, mobile-first indexing, 2023

Browsers call it "Not secure"

Since 2018, Chrome has labelled every page without HTTPS as "Not secure" next to your address.

On Google
HTTPS is one of the page experience signals Google uses alongside mobile-friendliness.

Sources: Search Engine Land, Google Search Central

It looks out of date

In a Stanford study, 46.1% of comments about whether a site was credible were about its visual design.

For your customers
A tired design makes a good business look closed, cheap or careless before anyone reads a word.

Source: Stanford web credibility research, via Lean Labs

Your reviews are hidden

85% of consumers are more likely to use a business after reading positive reviews.

For your customers
Old sites rarely show reviews at all, so the best proof you have never reaches the people deciding.

Source: BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026

Google can't tell what you do

Google says great, relevant content still matters more than any design signal.

On Google
One thin page with no services, areas or hours gives Google nothing to match to local searches like "plumber near me".

Source: Google Search Central

Questions owners ask us

Something else on your mind? Email hello@kromaloom.com and you'll hear back within a day.

Will I lose my Google rankings if you redesign my site?

No. We keep your existing page addresses or redirect every old address to its new page, keep the content that ranks, and resubmit your sitemap to Google after launch.

Is the concept really free?

Yes. We redesign your homepage as a concept at no cost and with no obligation, usually within 24 hours. You only pay if you want the full redesign.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. We build your new site on a private preview link. You pay once you've approved it, and then we put it live on your domain.

Can you redesign a site built on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or Shopify?

Yes. We can redesign it on the platform you already use, or move you to a faster, cheaper setup if that suits you better. We'll recommend one in your concept.

How can you redesign a website in 24 to 48 hours?

We use a tested process and our own design system, and your existing site already holds most of the content. Online store redesigns take 3 to 5 days.

Why are your prices so much lower than local agencies?

We're a small remote studio with no office, no account managers and no long meetings. You pay for design and build time, not overhead.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain and hosting stay in your own accounts, and every file is yours once you've paid.

Where are you based?

We're a remote studio based in India, working with clients in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom and the UAE. Calls are booked in your time zone.

Do you build Arabic or French versions?

Yes. A second-language version, such as Arabic for the UAE or French for Quebec, is available as an add-on at 50% of the package price.

Will the 3D effects slow my site down?

No. We use 3D and animation as a single accent, keep everything else light, and test every page on mobile speed before launch.
