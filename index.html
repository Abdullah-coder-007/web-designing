How it works

Five steps from first look to a site you own. You only pay once you've seen the concept and want the full build.

  1. We look at your current site

    We check it on a phone, test its speed, and note what's stopping visitors from contacting you.

  2. You get a free concept

    We design your new homepage and send you a preview. No cost, no obligation.

  3. You approve and pay half

    Like it? Pay 50% to start. We send a short form for your logo, photos, services and hours.

  4. We build it in about a week

    Every page, forms, search setup and speed tuning. Two rounds of changes are included.

  5. You go live and own it

    Pay the rest when you're happy. Your domain, hosting and files are yours, with a simple guide to editing.

Questions owners ask us

Is the concept really free?

Yes. We design a concept of your new homepage at no cost and with no obligation. You only pay if you want us to build the full site.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain and hosting stay in your own accounts, and every file transfers to you once the final payment is made.

Where are you based?

We're a remote studio based in India, working with clients in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom and the UAE. Calls are booked in your time zone.

Can you work with WordPress or Shopify?

Yes. We build on WordPress, Shopify, or as a fast static site, depending on how often you need to edit it yourself. We'll recommend one in your concept.

Do you build Arabic or French websites?

Yes. A second-language version, such as Arabic for the UAE or French for Quebec, is available as an add-on at 50% of the package price.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, a few photos, your services, hours and any reviews you'd like shown. If you don't have photos, we'll use properly licensed stock images.

How does payment work?

You pay 50% to start and the remaining 50% before launch, once you're happy with the site. We invoice in CAD, USD, GBP or AED.

Will the 3D effects slow my site down?

No. We use 3D and animation as a single accent, keep everything else light, and test every page on mobile speed before launch.
